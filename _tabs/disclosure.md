This blog is written by someone who does security research for a living-adjacent
living. That imposes rules on what can appear here, and it's fair to state them
up front rather than let readers guess.

## What I publish

- **Findings, only after they may be public.** A finding appears here when the
  program or vendor has resolved it *and* their disclosure policy permits
  publication, or when a coordinated disclosure window has elapsed under their
  stated terms. Both conditions, not either.
- **Method, freely.** How I scope, how I test, how I triage, how I decide to
  stop — described generically. Method posts describe what I do, never what I
  found or where I found it.
- **Tooling and engineering**, with no dependency on any engagement.
- **Opinion**, about the ecosystem rather than about individual programs.

## What I do not publish

- Anything about an open or undisclosed engagement.
- Program names, hostnames, internal identifiers, or credentials belonging to
  any target, disclosed or not, unless that program's own policy permits it.
- Screenshots that identify a target, an account, or a person.
- Findings from engagements where publication consent was never given, however
  long ago they happened.

## Corrections and redactions

If something here reveals more than it should — mine or someone else's — tell me
and I will correct or remove it, and say publicly that I did. I would rather
lose a post than a program's trust, and a target's trust.

## Responsible reporting

If you want to report a vulnerability in something I run, email me rather than
posting it publicly. I'll acknowledge within a few days and be honest about
whether I can fix it.
