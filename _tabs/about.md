I'm dotcael. I build software and I break it, mostly web apps, mostly on purpose.

By day I work on application development; in the margins I do security research
and bug bounty hunting, and I keep a growing pile of tooling that exists because
something annoyed me enough to fix it.

## What I write about

- **Guides**: things I worked out the hard way, written so the next person
  doesn't have to. Labs I've run, tools I've wired together, commands I got
  wrong before I got them right.
- **Notes**: shorter pieces: a build that broke in an interesting way, a design
  decision I'd defend, something in my workflow that turned out to matter.
- **Opinion**: the bug bounty ecosystem, tooling economics, and where the
  practice is headed. Arguments, stated as arguments.
- **Writeups**: findings, once the program says they can be public. See the
  [disclosure policy](/disclosure/) for exactly when that is.

## How I work

Local-first, measured, and allergic to hype. If I claim something, there's a
reproducible step behind it. If a result looks too good, I assume I broke
something before I assume I found something.
